Eliminating the 3 Critical Biases in Clinical AI

TrueState is an enterprise middleware stack that sits between raw hospital data streams (FHIR R4) and clinical predictions, guaranteeing fairness, causal debiasing, and conformal decision support.

Act 1: IV-OPIL
Observation Policy Bias

Disentangles hospital sampling frequency from patient health state.

Act 2: Proxy-SLCD
Selective Label Bias

Populates untested, high-risk marginalized patients using proxy controls.

Act 3: H-CDP + AIA
Conformal Decisions

Outputs 95% coverage valid action sets with test recommendations.
